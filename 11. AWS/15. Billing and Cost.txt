Pricing model:

    1. On-Demand Instances
    Pay by the hour/second with no long-term commitments. Perfect for unpredictable workloads 
    and getting started quickly.

    2. Reserved Instances
    Commit 1-3 years for big discounts up to 75%. Ideal for steady-state workloads with 
    predictable usage patterns.

    3. Spot Instances
    Bid for unused capacity, cheapest but interruptible. Great for fault-tolerant applications0
    and batch processing.

    4. Free Tier
    Limited free usage for new accounts (12 months). Essential for learning and testing before 
    production deployment.

Free tier:
    - Limited service with some limited type inside service also 
    - for 6 months
    - Good for testing

AWS billing tools:
    
    1. Billing dashboard: 
        - High level view of monthly charges, breakdown by service, region

    2. Cost explorer:
        - Analyze usage and spending patterns with detailed filtering and forecasting 
          capabilities

    3. Budget:
        - Define cost and usage threshold with custom alerts and automated actions
        - set budget to prevent overspending

    4. Billing alarms:
        - Get alerts via cloudwatch + SNS when cost exceed thresholds

    5. Cost Allocation Tags:
        - tag resources (team:backend, env:prod) so Cost Explorer/CUR can break spend down by 
          who owns it. Without tagging discipline, cost attribution in a real org is chaos
